01 / Gravity

Gravity of Closed Strings

What theory of gravity follows naturally from closed strings?

Working hypothesis

Three fields, one gravitational geometry. We investigate DFT as its natural formulation, including non-Riemannian geometries.

Research themes

Geometry 2010–

Doubled-yet-gauged spacetime; non-Riemannian backgrounds.

Unified Field Equation 2018–

Einstein Double Field Equation for the NS–NS sector.

Propagation 2025–

O(D,D)-covariant wave operator; closed-string modes.

Phenomenology 2022–

Post-Newtonian tests, cosmology and black holes.

The challenge

Which observations could distinguish closed-string gravity from general relativity?
